Questions People Usually Ask
A few things worth knowing
Do I need to understand AI or prompt engineering?

No. Start by describing the result you want in ordinary language. AI4U helps turn that into a clearer route and instructions.

Does AI4U do the work for me?

AI4U helps choose a starting tool, prepares instructions, and guides the next steps. The selected AI or platform performs the task. You remain in control of what gets used, changed, or saved.

Is the recommendation guaranteed to be the best?

No. It is a reasoned starting recommendation based on the task details, task stage, and risk. Platform capabilities and pricing change. Review the explanation, compare alternatives when needed, and record the real outcome.

What does the percentage in a route result mean?

It is a rule-based routing guidance indicator—not a measured probability of success, a benchmark of model accuracy, or a guarantee that the output is correct.

Can I use AI4U for things other than software?

Yes. Writing, research, product comparisons, learning, creative work, planning, business processes, automation, and software projects are all useful starting points.

Can I upload my files to AI4U?

The current Route Engine uses the information you enter in its fields; it does not itself read file attachments. When the recommended AI supports uploads, attach your documents directly in that AI tool and include their contents in the task context.

Where does the Ledger save my information?

Without a connected and signed-in Supabase account, records are saved in this browser. If Supabase is configured and you are signed in, supported records can sync to your account. Browser-only data does not automatically follow you to another device.

What if I disagree with the recommendation?

Change the task details, stage, or risk and route it again. You can also compare alternatives in Platform Matrix. The recommendation is there to help you decide—not to take the decision away from you.

Do I need to save every task?

No. Save tasks when the outcome or lesson will be useful later. Record a real result only after you have checked it; do not mark a task as successful just because an AI produced an answer.

Can I trust AI output for important decisions?

Do not rely on AI alone for medical, legal, financial, safety, or other high-stakes decisions. Check original sources and use a qualified professional where appropriate.

Plain-English Glossary
A quick translation of the tool names
Route Engine

The starting point. Describe your task and receive a suggested tool, an explanation, and a way to check the result.

Task Stage

Where you are in the work: starting an idea, making a first version, repairing something, launching, automating, or evaluating.

Risk Level

How carefully the work needs to be checked. A casual brainstorm is different from a public launch or a decision with serious consequences.

Platform Matrix

A comparison view of the AI and software platforms AI4U tracks and the kinds of work they are suited to.

Prompt Gen

The instruction writer. It turns task details into a structured prompt you can copy into the selected AI assistant.

Proof Chain

A place to record evidence that a result passed the checks that matter for that particular task.

Ledger

Your record of what you tried, which tool you used, what happened, and what you learned.

Artifact

The actual thing produced: a document, image, report, website, workflow, code change, or other deliverable.
